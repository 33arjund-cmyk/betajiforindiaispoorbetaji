Homelessness & Poverty in India

A Text-Based Awareness App

Introduction

India is home to millions of people who do not have permanent shelter. Homelessness is closely linked to poverty, unemployment, and lack of access to basic services.

Daily Life of Homeless People

Many homeless individuals sleep on sidewalks, under bridges, or near railway stations. They often lack clean drinking water, toilets, and healthcare.

Daily survival depends on begging, daily-wage labor, or charity. Food is uncertain, and illness often goes untreated.

Causes of Homelessness

  • Extreme poverty
  • Unemployment or low wages
  • Migration from villages to cities
  • Natural disasters
  • Lack of affordable housing

Impact on Children

Homeless children often miss education and are exposed to hunger, child labor, and health risks. Without support, the cycle of poverty continues.

Why Awareness Matters

Understanding homelessness helps society push for better policies, shelters, employment programs, and education opportunities.
