Homelessness & Poverty in India

Text-Only Awareness App

Introduction

India has many people who live without permanent homes. Homelessness is linked to poverty, unemployment, and lack of support.

Daily Life

Homeless people often sleep on roadsides, under bridges, or near stations. They struggle to get food, water, and medical help.

Reasons for Homelessness

  • Poverty
  • Lack of jobs
  • Migration to cities
  • Natural disasters
  • No affordable housing

Children and Poverty

Many homeless children cannot go to school and face hunger and illness. This keeps them trapped in poverty.

Conclusion

Awareness can help bring change through better policies, shelters, education, and employment opportunities.
