Homelessness & Poverty in India

Interactive Text-Only Awareness App

Introduction

India has millions of people who live without permanent shelter. Homelessness is caused by poverty, unemployment, migration, disasters, and lack of affordable housing.

This app uses only text and simple graphs to explain how serious the problem is and why action is necessary.

Key Statistics (Estimates)

  • 1.7–2 million people are homeless in India
  • 65+ million people live in slums or temporary housing
  • 30–40% of urban residents live in informal settlements
  • 1 in 5 urban workers earns below basic living cost
  • 50%+ homeless people rely on daily-wage labor
  • Homeless people are 3–4× more likely to be seriously ill
  • Millions of children miss school due to poverty
  • Life expectancy of homeless people is far lower

Impact of Homelessness

Homelessness affects not only individuals but society as a whole.

  • Higher disease spread
  • Increased healthcare burden
  • Unsafe cities and public spaces
  • Lost economic productivity
  • Generational poverty

Why We Must Fix This

  • Housing is a basic human need
  • Healthy citizens build strong economies
  • Educated children mean a better future
  • Prevention is cheaper than crisis management
  • Reducing poverty improves safety and dignity

Ignoring homelessness costs more than fixing it. Long-term solutions benefit everyone.

Possible Solutions

  • Affordable housing programs
  • Night shelters and sanitation access
  • Free education for homeless children
  • Skill training and stable jobs
  • Support NGOs and community efforts

With proper planning and compassion, homelessness can be reduced.
